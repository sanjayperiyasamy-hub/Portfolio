ABOUT

I'm currently studying AI and building most of my projects end to end — frontend, backend, and the database underneath. Most of what I build starts as a real problem someone actually has: a tuition center that needed attendance tracked properly, a hotel that wanted table-side ordering without an app, a friend's events business that needed a site that looked like the prices it was charging.

The thread that connects it: I like taking something messy — a workflow, a form, a folder of paper records — and turning it into something that just works. Lately that's extended into Kadhai AI Studio, a tool I'm building for Tamil film writers, because I think the same instinct applies to storytelling as it does to software.
